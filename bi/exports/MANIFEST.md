## What each extract feeds

- **`01_open_pipeline_deals.csv`** (01 Pipeline Health): stage bars, aging buckets, momentum split, all filters.
- **`01_funnel_conversion.csv`** (01 Pipeline Health): stage to stage conversion rates.
- **`01_coverage_by_segment.csv`** (01 Pipeline Health): coverage ratio KPI and its conditional color.
- **`02_rep_attainment.csv`** (02 Rep Performance and Ramp): attainment histogram and the sortable rep table.
- **`02_ramp_by_cohort.csv`** (02 Rep Performance and Ramp): ramp curves by cohort.
- **`02_win_rate_by_tenure.csv`** (02 Rep Performance and Ramp): win rate by tenure band.
- **`03_channel_efficiency.csv`** (03 Channel Efficiency): return on spend bars, cost against revenue scatter, volume.
- **`03_cycle_by_source.csv`** (03 Channel Efficiency): cycle length by source.
- **`04_data_quality_checks.csv`** (04 Data Quality Monitor): ranked issue list and the dollars exposed headline.
- **`04_flagged_records.csv`** (04 Data Quality Monitor): drill down work list.
